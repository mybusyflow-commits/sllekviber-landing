SleekViber design credits

React Bits by David Haz:
Official ScrollStack, GridMotion and GlareHover templates, restyled for SleekViber.
CardSwap remains available in the project; it is no longer used in the hero.
Adapted Magnet, MagnetLines, ScrollFloat, TextPressure, SplitText and PixelTransition.
Changes include local fonts, semantic controls, reduced motion, visibility gating,
React 19 compatibility, event-driven updates and a shared Lenis scroll owner.
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits
License: /credits/REACT-BITS-LICENSE.txt

GSAP and Lenis power scroll and reveal motion. Lucide supplies interface icons.
Clash Display and General Sans are locally hosted; font licenses are retained in app/fonts.
Typography specimens, route linework and state-responsive geometry are original content
within the React Bits templates. They are illustrative design studies.
Oryzo by Lusion was a visual pacing and interaction reference. No Oryzo assets or copy are used.
The current hero adapts Aceternity's free Hero Section One composition and entrance
choreography with SleekViber typography, copy, navigation and fixed survey action:
https://ui.aceternity.com/components/hero-sections-free
Aceternity license: https://ui.aceternity.com/licence
Its immersive background uses React Bits ColorBends' original shader, customized
with the brand palette, bounded pointer influence, a pause control, reduced motion,
30fps and resolution caps, offscreen/hidden-page suspension, and static fallback.
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/tree/main/src/ts-default/Backgrounds/ColorBends
No floating models are mounted by the current hero.
